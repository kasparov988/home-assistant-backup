Privacy Policy

Home Assistant Backup is a personal, non-commercial application used by its owner to store Home Assistant backups in Google Drive.

Google Drive Access

The application requests Google Drive access to create and manage backup files associated with this application.

Data Storage

Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. These backups may contain Home Assistant configuration and device information.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google user data with advertisers or other third parties.

Access Revocation

The owner can revoke Google Drive access through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
